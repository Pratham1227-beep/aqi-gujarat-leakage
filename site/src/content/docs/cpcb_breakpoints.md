## Notes

- AQI is the **maximum** of all available sub-indices.
- AQI is valid only if at least 3 pollutants have valid sub-indices **and** at least one of PM2.5 or PM10 is present.
- CPCB uses 24-hourly average values for PM2.5, PM10, SO2, NO2, NH3; 8-hourly rolling maximum for CO and O3.
- For the 8-hourly pollutants, the daily AQI uses the maximum 8-hour rolling maximum of the day.
